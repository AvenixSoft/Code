Live network probes

Workflow

From URL to evidence in three steps

No agents, no DNS changes, no access to your infrastructure. Everything runs from the browser against the public surface.

01

Paste the target

Any public hostname. The engine normalizes it, probes reachability and picks the fastest relay for your network.

system://scan --target=example.com --profile=deep
02

Live pipeline runs

DNS, fetch, header grades, HTML analysis and path probes execute in parallel. You watch every request in the terminal.

8 stages · 4 parallel workers
03

Report with evidence

Layer scores, findings ranked by severity, raw evidence per finding, and a full JSON export for your pipeline.

export: system-report-<host>.json

Data sources

Twelve sources, zero black boxes

The engine fans out to public, documented services and to your target itself. Every request is logged in the terminal during the scan, with latency.

Source health is checked liveopen Status in the footer to run a check from your browser

Requests to your target are relayed through public CORS proxies when direct access is blocked by the browser. The report always shows which path produced each result.
